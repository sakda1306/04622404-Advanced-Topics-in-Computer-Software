[หมวด: Premier League Club Nicknames]
Q: Which Premier League club do Thai fans call ผีแดง (also แมนยู), and why is it called that?
A: Manchester United. ผีแดง means "Red Devils" in Thai. Manchester United took the nickname "Red Devils" in the 1960s under manager Matt Busby, borrowing it from Salford rugby league club, whom the French press called "Les Diables Rouges" on a 1934 tour of France. A red devil appears on the club crest.

Q: Which Premier League club do Thai fans call หงส์แดง, and why is it called that?
A: Liverpool. หงส์แดง means "red swan" in Thai: Thai fans named the club after the Liver bird on its crest, the symbol of the city of Liverpool, and its red shirts. In English Liverpool are called "The Reds". Rival fans sometimes tease them as เป็ดแดง ("red duck").

Q: Which Premier League club do Thai fans call เรือใบ or เรือใบสีฟ้า, and why is it called that?
A: Manchester City. เรือใบสีฟ้า means "sky-blue sailing ship" in Thai: the club crest shows a ship, a symbol of Manchester and its Ship Canal, and the team plays in sky blue. In English Manchester City are called "The Citizens" (or "Cityzens") and "The Sky Blues".

Q: Which Premier League club do Thai fans call ไก่เดือยทอง, and why is it called that?
A: Tottenham Hotspur. ไก่เดือยทอง means "cockerel with golden spurs" in Thai: the Tottenham crest shows a cockerel standing on a football. The name Hotspur comes from Sir Henry Percy, known as Harry Hotspur, whose family owned land in Tottenham, and fighting cockerels wore spurs. In English the club is called "Spurs".

Q: Which Premier League club do Thai fans call ขุนค้อน, and why is it called that?
A: West Ham United. ขุนค้อน means "lords of the hammer" in Thai. West Ham began in 1895 as Thames Ironworks FC, the works team of a shipbuilding company, and two crossed hammers are on the club crest. In English West Ham are called "The Hammers" or "The Irons".
